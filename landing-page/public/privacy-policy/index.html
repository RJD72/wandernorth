Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Wander North is a route-planning and travel discovery application developed by WebSmith Creations. This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, used, stored, and shared when you use the Wander North mobile application or the wandernorth.tech website.

Mobile app information

Wander North may request access to your device's current location when you choose the Current Location feature. Location is used to establish a starting point, calculate routes, and find places or points of interest along or near a route. Wander North requests foreground location access and does not intentionally track your location in the background.

Information you enter in the app, including starting locations, destinations, search terms, selected points of interest, and route waypoints, may be sent to third-party mapping and location services so that Wander North can provide routes, place information, and search results.

Wander North currently uses services provided by:

  • Google Maps Platform, including Routes and Places services.
  • TomTom location and search services.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Normal internet requests may also expose technical information such as an IP address and device or network information to those providers.

Saved trips

Trips you choose to save in the mobile app are stored locally on your device. Wander North does not currently synchronize saved trips to a WebSmith Creations server or user account. Saved trip information may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the app's local data, or uninstall the app.

Accounts and sign-in

Wander North does not currently require users to create a mobile-app account or sign in.

Website and early-access information

The wandernorth.tech website may collect information that you voluntarily submit through the Wander North early-access or research form. This may include your first name, email address, province or region, travel preferences, desired outcomes, pricing preferences, and whether you are interested in early testing.

Early-access submissions are used to understand interest in Wander North, prioritize product development, contact people who request meaningful development or testing updates, and evaluate how the product may be used. This information is not sold.

Website analytics

wandernorth.tech may use Google Analytics to measure website usage and interactions. The site's analytics configuration is intended to avoid advertising personalization and does not intentionally send form email addresses to Google Analytics. Analytics providers may process technical data such as browser, device, network, and general usage information according to their own policies.

The Wander North mobile application does not currently include third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.

Advertising

Wander North does not currently contain third-party advertising.

Sharing of information

Wander North does not sell or rent personal information. Information required to provide routing, location search, point-of-interest discovery, website analytics, hosting, and early-access functionality may be processed by service providers used to operate those features.

Data security

Network requests made by Wander North and wandernorth.tech use encrypted HTTPS connections where supported. Saved mobile-app trips are stored locally within the application's device storage. Users should not use saved-trip fields to store confidential or highly sensitive personal information.

Data retention and deletion

WebSmith Creations does not currently maintain server-side user accounts or a server-side database of saved Wander North mobile-app trips. Locally stored saved trips can be removed through the app's saved-trip controls, by clearing the application's local data, or by uninstalling the application.

Early-access information submitted through wandernorth.tech may be retained while Wander North is in development and while it remains useful for product research, tester communication, or related business records. You may request deletion of information you submitted by contacting the email address below.

Information processed by third-party service providers may be retained according to those providers' respective policies.

Children's privacy

Wander North is not specifically directed toward children under 13 and does not knowingly solicit personal information from children through mobile-app accounts because the app currently does not provide account registration.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Wander North changes or as new features and services are introduced. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
